# Promorg 1.2.0

Draft notes for the GitHub release `v1.2.0`. Paste everything below the line into the release description.

---

**Promorg 1.2.0: make your own promos, and handle the music sent to you.**

**Install:** download `Promorg-1.2.0-Setup.exe` and run it, or use `Promorg-1.2.0-Standalone.exe`, which needs no
install. If the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime isn't on the PC, the installer offers to download and install it from
Microsoft. Neither file is code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Updating**
- **From Promorg 1.0.0 or 0.5.4:** **Settings › About & updates › Check for updates**, then **Download and
  install**. The update is checked against its release signature before it runs. Your mail, releases and settings
  are kept.
- **From Promo Organiser 0.2.0:** the same, in Promo Organiser. Your mail, releases, settings and saved passwords
  move over to Promorg on its first start.
  - **Close Promo Organiser before running the Standalone** (or the installer, if you run it by hand). If it's still
    open, Promorg asks you to close it first. If you can't see its window, end PromoOrganiser.exe in Task Manager
    (Ctrl+Shift+Esc).
  - **Re-pin Promorg on the taskbar.** A pin made for Promo Organiser points at the old program, which the update
    removes.
  - **Don't go back to Promo Organiser 0.2.0** after Promorg's first start: your data has moved, so it would open empty.
- **The database upgrade is one-way.** Once 1.2.0 has started, an older Promorg (an older Standalone, or a copy
  on another PC sharing the folder) can't open your data: update every copy you use. 1.2.0 keeps a copy of your
  database from before the upgrade, `promorg.db.before-schema-10.<date>.bak`, beside it (see Back up and restore in
  the README).

What came in 1.0 (cards, your accent colour, the heading row, the details pane's new layout) is in the
[1.0.0 release](https://github.com/geodnb/promorg-releases/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**New in 1.2.0**

- **Create a Promo.** A new view on the left for your own releases, to send to a distributor, a promo service, a
  store such as Bandcamp, or a label as a demo.
  - **Release**: title, artist, label, catalogue number, UPC/EAN, release date, genre, ℗ and © lines, the promo
    text and the artwork (Promorg keeps a copy and tells you its size).
  - **Tracks**: mix, artists, remixers, ISRC, BPM, key, length, explicit, songwriters, producers, file name.
  - **Artist**: a profile saved once for all the artist's promos: Spotify and Apple Music IDs (paste the artist's
    link), store and social links, business email, bio, press photos. Music-related details only: nothing personal.
  - **Files and sending**: where the files are kept (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, SoundCloud, WeTransfer…)
    and where it's going (Triple Vision, Label Worx, DistroKid, Inflyte, FATdrop, Bandcamp and more, or **Your
    services…** to add your own). **What it still needs** lists what that kind of place asks for: a distributor
    wants every code and credit and 3000 × 3000 artwork, a label demo little more than a listening link.
    **Copy all details** or **Save details…** (text, or a CSV with a row a track).
  - **Emails**: drafts made from the promo: a promo to DJs and channels, a demo to a label, a release to a
    distributor, a pitch to playlists, blogs or press, an "out now" and a follow-up. Change anything; then **Open
    in Gmail**, **Open in email app**, copy, or save as an email file.
  - **Notes** on each promo and each artist.
- **Submissions.** Tracks sent to you for your channel or label, and music on your PC, in one list.
  - Found in **Direct** and **Other**: a music file attached, or a SoundCloud, Dropbox, WeTransfer or similar link
    in an email about music. Newsletters and machine-sent mail are left out.
  - **Scan this PC…** says exactly what it will do before it starts: it looks in your folders (or every drive) for
    music of a minute or more, skips Windows', programs' and games' folders and short sounds, reads only names,
    sizes and tags, and adds only the folders you tick. Or **Add folder…** and **Add files…**.
  - A track too big to keep with its email (over 5 MB) is listed by name: **Download from mailbox…** fetches just
    that file when you ask (read-only, as a sync connects; the email isn't marked as read), or download it yourself
    and add it, and it joins its submission.
  - **A player**: play through the list, with previous, next, position and volume. See what the email says and what
    the file's own tags say (length, format, BPM, key, genre, label…), and keep your notes.
  - **Accept**, **Maybe** or **Reject**. Accepting drafts a reply saying you like it and asking for more music;
    rejecting drafts a polite no with your feedback. **Reply templates…** sets your channel or label's name and both
    emails. Open the reply in Gmail (from the mailbox it came to) or your email app, copy it, or save it.
  - **Export playlist…** saves the tracks on your PC as an .m3u8.
- **Notes on contacts.** Keep your own notes on each artist, label, distributor, platform or sender in Contacts;
  they show in the list and the search finds them.

Promorg still never sends email, signs in to music services or uploads anything: it opens their websites and your
email for you.

**Also new since 1.0** (from 1.1.0, which was folded into this release)

- **The counts on the left show what's new.** The numbers on Promos, Messages, Direct and Other now count what
  arrived since you last opened that view, so opening it clears its count. After updating they start empty.
  - **Right-click a view** on the left for **Clear count**, or, in Messages, Direct and Other, **Mark all as
    handled** (**Dismiss all**), which asks first and covers the mailbox tab you're on.
  - **Settings › Appearance › Counts on the left** can make them count everything not dealt with yet, as
    before, or turn them off.
- **Several emails at once.** In Messages, Direct and Other, Ctrl+click or Shift+click to select several emails
  (Ctrl+A for all), and **Mark handled** (**Dismiss**) marks them all. **Mark all shown handled** is now a button.
- **Open in Gmail.** Select an email in Messages, Direct or Other and click **Open in Gmail** (or right-click it)
  to open that email itself in your browser, in the right Google account: for its attachments, to reply, or to see
  it as it was sent. Yahoo, iCloud, AOL, Fastmail and Zoho mailboxes open their webmail.
- **Open link opens the email's link.** In Messages and Direct, when an email has no promo link (an artist sending
  their mailchi.mp page, say), **Open link** opens its first web link, and **Create release from this** gives that
  link to the release. Enter or a double-click opens a promo link, or else reads the email in its own window.
- **Calmer selected tabs.** A selected tab is a soft wash with an outline instead of a solid block of colour: a status
  tab in its status's colour (below); a mailbox tab, and the tabs in Settings, in your Windows accent colour, with a
  mailbox tab a plain lighter grey in dark mode. In a Windows contrast theme they use your Highlight colour.
- **Contacts.** A new view on the left: the artists, labels, distributors, promo platforms and email senders in your
  promos, each kind on its own tab, with how many promos (and how many still New), when the latest arrived, their
  email addresses and their promos. **Show in Promos** shows just that contact's promos.
- **Pick the track.** In a setlist's **Track** column, pick which track you'll play from each promo: the list offers
  the tracks in the promo's emails (its tracklist), or type your own. It's what the tracklist and playlist use.
- **Setlists as playlists.** Setlist › **Export…** saves a playlist for DJ software (.m3u8, offered first) that
  rekordbox, Serato, Traktor, Engine DJ and VirtualDJ can import, a tracklist ("Artist - Track", one per line), or a
  CSV file. For the playlist, pick the folder where you keep the promos' audio files: Promorg finds each track by its
  file name and tells you which it couldn't find.
- **A lighter look.** Buttons are slightly raised, sections have a soft shadow instead of a hard outline, and lists
  have no lines between rows: the row under the pointer or selected is a soft rounded band.
- **Status colours.** Each status tab has its own colour, and the selected one is tinted in it: **Rejected** red,
  **Uploaded** green, **New** dark orange, **Reviewed** light blue, **Shortlisted** purple and **Scheduled** yellow.
- **"Listened" is now "Reviewed".** Same status, a clearer name (still Ctrl+2); your releases keep their status.
- **Check for a newer SQLite.** Settings › About & updates shows the SQLite version Promorg keeps your data with, and
  can ask sqlite.org whether a newer one is out. SQLite is built into Promorg, so a newer one arrives with a Promorg
  update. This release has SQLite 3.53.3, the newest packaged for .NET so far.
- **Received and Mailbox together.** With several mailboxes, the Promos list now shows both the received date and
  the Mailbox column in a normal-sized window (about 1500 px wide or more). In a narrower window it hides Platform
  first, then the received date on the All mailboxes tab, or the Mailbox column on a mailbox's own tab.

**Also fixed in 1.2.0** (from a full independent review)
- **Updates:** the installer that runs is always the one whose signature was checked, even if another program tries to
  swap its folder at the last moment; an update for an all-users install asks for administrator rights at once.
- **Mail in older character sets** (Outlook's windows-1252 with curly apostrophes or a euro sign, Cyrillic, Japanese,
  Chinese, Korean) is read with the right characters, and such a label mailout is a promo again. Stored mail is re-read
  once after the update.
- **An email made of very many links** can no longer stall a sync or freeze the window.
- **Typing is never lost:** pressing Enter in Add promo or the mailbox form, or a shortcut such as Alt+1 to Alt+8 or
  Alt+N, saves what you were typing first.
- **Promos:** **It's a promo** asks about unsaved changes first; every way of opening a ★ release asks before opening
  its link; a status set in the details pane clears the ● mark; the list keeps the keyboard after a change.
- **Setlist** no longer rebuilds itself while you're typing a track or note during a sync.
- **Small screens:** the left rail fits a 1080p laptop at 150% (or scrolls), the window opens fully on screen, and tall
  dialogs scroll.
- **Submissions** copies an emailed track and reads its tags only when you play or save it; a broken audio file can't
  stop a scan or keep Promorg from closing or starting.
- **Backups** are checked before they count, and **Help › Backing up and restoring** says how to restore one.
- **Licences:** Settings › About › **Third-party notices** lists the open-source software Promorg includes.

**Fixed since 1.0** (from 1.1.0)
- Removing a release no longer hides another label's promo that uses the same short catalogue number ("EP01").
- A cover you downloaded is kept when you click **It's a promo**.
- **Mark handled** / **Dismiss** says **Mark not handled** / **Undo dismiss** on an email that's already handled, so
  a second press doesn't quietly bring it back.
- Several selected emails stay selected while a sync refreshes the list.
- If you change a mailbox's server or login, Promorg asks for the password again rather than sending the saved one
  to the new server.
- Dark theme: no white block in Setlist before you pick a setlist, and the email text no longer sits in a grey box.
- Settings has a **Close** button (Esc), and a few labels read better ("Move to Promos", "1 promo").

**Known issues**
- Starting Promorg a second time sometimes says "Promorg is already running…" even though the first copy is fine.
  This can happen when it's started from a launcher or script, or while the first copy is still starting. Switch to
  the Promorg window that's already open.
- If a backup or security program briefly holds Promorg's lock file, Promorg may also say it's already running when
  it isn't. Try again in a moment.
- The first start after updating to 1.2.0 re-reads your stored mail once, in the background (so mail in older
  character sets shows correctly). A large mailbox, or many Promo-Cloud or Promoly emails, makes it slower.
- Promo-Cloud tracklists that name versions in unusual ways can give a wrong artist, or none. Examples are
  "Sunrise Run - Kay Vee Edit", or a track that's just called "Original". An artist worked out from a tracklist is
  always marked ⚠ to check: correct it, or click **They're right**.

**Files**
- `Promorg-1.2.0-Setup.exe`: the installer.
- `Promorg-1.2.0-Standalone.exe`: the standalone app (no install, .NET included).
- `.sha256`: each file's SHA-256, for checking by hand.
- `.sig`: each file's release signature, which the app checks before installing an update.
- `PromoOrganiser-1.2.0-Setup.exe` and its `.sig`: the same installer under the old name, only so that Promo
  Organiser 0.2.0 finds the update by itself. Download the `Promorg-` files.
